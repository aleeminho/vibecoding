# DUO — Guidebook surface brief

## Scope and mode
Read. One A2-landscape sheet: `guidebook/duo-guidebook.html` rendered to PDF +
PNG. Not part of the app; it is the manual that travels with it.

## Audience and job
The operator first (complete walkthrough: login, scan, split, send, collect,
report), and second the friends who receive a nota or a payment link and need to
know what to do with it. The sheet must be readable at a wall and printable at a
copy shop; every step findable in seconds.

## Chosen direction
Extension of the committed kwitansi world: the manual is printed on the same
form. White sheet on the grey desk, orange only as rules and as the attention
ink, pre-printed labels, screenshots as exhibits pinned to the page, and marks
(state legend) printed the way the app prints them.

## Memorable moment
The legend strip: the pad's own grammar (dashed/half/pressed stamp square,
lettered LUNAS, "Perlu dicek") printed once as a reading key, so the guide
teaches the marks before the steps.

## Constraints
- Indonesian, informal voice matching the app's copy.
- No webfonts, no shadows, no translucency, no capsules — the app's bans hold.
- Screenshots are real captures of the current build.
- One sheet. No second page, no fold-out.

## Unresolved decisions
- The exact set of screens shown in the flow band (six fit; more would become a
  contact sheet).
- Whether "Yang sengaja nggak ada" belongs on the sheet (kept: it prevents
  feature requests for netting/push/signup).

## Direction contract
THESIS: The manual is a printed form from the same pad as the app — one big
sheet read top to bottom as the journey of one bill — and it refuses the
screenshot contact sheet and the marketing flyer.

OWN-WORLD: The committed kwitansi materials: desk grey, white sheets, hairline
rules, stamp-orange rules under the masthead and above the footer, pre-printed
labels at 11px, tabular money, mono serials, the Dua Ply mark. Screenshots are
exhibits on white paper with a 1px frame, never floating.

STORY: The reader follows one bill from the photo to "semua lunas", learns what
each tab does, what to do when a gate refuses, what the app deliberately does
not do, and how to read the pad's state marks.

FIRST VIEWPORT (the whole sheet): masthead with the Dua Ply mark, "Panduan
Pakai", the tagline and the one-line promise; a six-step flow band with
screenshots and drawn arrows; below it three columns — the tabs explained, the
troubleshooting table, and payers' etiquette plus what is deliberately absent;
the legend strip and the footer rule at the bottom.

FORM: Local extension of the committed world; no new identity, no seed roll.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the
finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its
provenance.
